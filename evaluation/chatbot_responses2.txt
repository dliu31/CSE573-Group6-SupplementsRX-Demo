The user is asking about the health benefits and conditions that calcium can help with. : Calcium is beneficial for several conditions. It is essential for bone health, nerve transmission, and muscle contraction. It can help manage renal disease by preventing phosphate absorption and may play a role in preventing cardiovascular disease, hypertension, and stroke by potentially lowering blood pressure. Calcium may also lower inflammation markers and has shown promise in cancer prevention, particularly colorectal cancer. Furthermore, adequate calcium intake is associated with a lower risk of developing type 2 diabetes and may be used to treat alcohol withdrawal symptoms.

The user is asking about the potential health risks associated with excessive selenium intake. : Excessive selenium intake can negate potential benefits and even become adverse, especially above 70 mcg daily. The risk of excessive intake has increased significantly with the proliferation of dietary supplements and fortified foods, creating multiple pathways.
The user is asking for a description of selenosis and its harmful effects on the body. : Selenosis, or selenium toxicity, occurs when the body ingests excessive amounts of selenium, leading to harmful effects. While selenium is essential, high levels (above 2000 mcg/L) can paradoxically increase cellular oxidation by promoting reactive oxygen species (ROS) formation and toxic nitrous oxide. Vitamin C may reduce selenium's cytotoxicity.
